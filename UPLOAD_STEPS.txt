Legendary Twelve v22 - upload steps (GitHub Pages)

1. Upload EVERY file in this folder to the root of your repository, replacing the old ones:
   index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, the 4 icon .png files,
   dict_1.json to dict_10.json (360,000-word dictionary and spelling list, about 16 MB),
   and the 4 NEW language pack files: lang_freq.json, lang_ant.json, lang_pron_1.json, lang_pron_2.json (about 3.7 MB).
   Tip: on a phone, use "Add file > Upload files" and select them all at once.
2. Wait a minute for Pages to rebuild, then open the site and reload once.
3. First open on each device downloads everything once (Wi-Fi recommended). After that it works offline.
4. Tell any assistant your name once ("my name is ...") and they will greet you by name every time.
