Legendary Twelve v21 - upload steps (GitHub Pages)

1. Upload EVERY file in this folder to the root of your repository, replacing the old ones:
   index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, the 4 icon .png files, and the 10 dictionary files
   dict_1.json to dict_10.json (about 16 MB total, they hold the 360,000-word dictionary and spelling list).
   Tip: on a phone, use "Add file > Upload files" and select them all at once.
2. Wait a minute for Pages to rebuild, then open the site and reload once.
3. First open on each device downloads the dictionary once (needs internet, Wi-Fi recommended). After that it works offline.
4. Tell any assistant your name once ("my name is ...") and they will greet you by name every time.
