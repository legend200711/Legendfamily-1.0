THE LEGENDARY TWELVE - upload steps (phone only, free)

FIRST TIME
1. In Chrome on your phone, open github.com and sign in.
2. Tap + > New repository. Name it legendary-twelve. Set it to Public. Tap Create repository.
3. Tap "uploading an existing file". Unzip this package first, then select ALL files in it
   (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the four .png icons). Tap Commit changes.
4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under Branch pick main and folder / (root). Tap Save.
5. Wait about a minute. Your site opens at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/legendary-twelve/
6. Open it in Chrome > menu > Add to Home screen.

UPDATING LATER
Open the repository > Add file > Upload files > upload index.html and sw.js (they replace the old ones)
> Commit changes. Close the app fully and open it again.

NOTES
- Just talk to the assistants. They learn facts you tell them ("A capacitor is..."), your name and likes,
  and if they don't know something they ask you and remember your answer. Say "forget that" to undo.
- Everything runs offline in your phone. No accounts, no cloud AI.
- What you teach the assistants is saved in the browser on your phone. Use Control > Export all
  now and then and save the text somewhere safe.
- Keep the repository Public only if you are okay with the app itself being public.
  It contains no personal data; your teaching stays on your phone.
