THE LEGENDARY TWELVE - update (version 5: voice diagnostics)

To update your GitHub Pages site:
1. Open your repository on github.com in your phone browser.
2. Tap Add file > Upload files.
3. Upload these two files from this zip (they replace the old ones):
   index.html   and   sw.js
4. Tap Commit changes. Wait about a minute.
5. Close the app fully and open it again to load the new version.

VOICE TEST
Open any assistant > Settings > Assistant > Test voice.
You should hear a beep, then a voice. The page then shows what happened
(for example "voice started" or "error not-allowed").
- Beep but no voice: your phone has no text-to-speech engine enabled.
  Settings > System > Languages > Text-to-speech > pick Google.
- No beep either: turn up MEDIA volume, turn off silent mode, and open the
  site directly in Chrome (not inside another app).
